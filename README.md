# Awesome AI Video Trend Generators [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of single-purpose AI video generators built around viral TikTok and Reels trends. Each tool takes one or two photos and turns them into a short clip of a specific trend, without prompts or editing.

Every entry has one neutral sentence. The facts (video length, resolution, free options, whether an account is needed) were read from each tool's own website on **2026-10-15**. Prices are left out on purpose because they change every few days on these sites. Check the site itself before you pay.

## Disclosure

This list is maintained by the operator of two of the tools listed here: **zombie-couple.com** and **tiptoeing-in-my-jordans.com**. Those two entries are marked *(maintainer's site)*. Entries are sorted alphabetically by domain, and every tool is described with the same kind of facts. No entry is paid or sponsored, and there are no affiliate links.

## Zombie couple / zombie love story

The trend: one partner turns into a zombie, the other raises a gun, cannot shoot, and hugs them instead.

- [ai-zombie.com](https://ai-zombie.com/) - 15-second zombie couple videos in 480p or 720p; its FAQ says there is no free option.
- [aizombietrend.com](https://aizombietrend.com/) - 15-second 720p videos, sign-in with Google or an email code, and one free remake within 7 days if the faces come out mixed up.
- [videozombiecouple.com](https://videozombiecouple.com/) - 12-second 720×1280 videos, with three free two-frame still previews a day on a free account.
- [zombie-couple-video.com](https://zombie-couple-video.com/) - 27-second 1080p videos with no account needed; each video takes about 20 to 30 minutes.
- [zombie-couple.com](https://zombie-couple.com/) - 10-second vertical video with sound from two photos, in 480p or 720p; you sign in when you pay. *(maintainer's site)*
- [zombieaitrend.com](https://zombieaitrend.com/) - 25-second 720p videos made of a 10-second scene plus a 15-second memory segment; no account needed (email optional).
- [zombieaitrend.org](https://zombieaitrend.org/) - 15-second videos in 480p or 720p; requires an account and has no free trial.
- [zombiecouple.org](https://zombiecouple.org/) - 10- or 15-second videos in 720p, or 1080p in its HD mode, with three free still scene previews per account.
- [zombiecoupleai.org](https://zombiecoupleai.org/) - 10-second videos; the free sign-up credit makes a zombie filter photo, not a video.
- [zombiecoupleaivideo.online](https://zombiecoupleaivideo.online/ai-zombie-hug) - 15-second zombie hug videos in 480p or 720p; failed renders are refunded automatically.
- [zombietrend.org](https://zombietrend.org/) - 8-, 12- or 15-second videos, with a free watermarked scene preview once a day and no sign-up for the preview.
- [zombietrendvideo.com](https://zombietrendvideo.com/) - About 15-second 768p videos with guest checkout for a single video; its page said generation was temporarily paused on the check date.
- [zombievideo.org](https://zombievideo.org/zombie-love-story) - Zombie love story videos from 5 to 15 seconds (12 by default) in 480p, 720p or 1080p; needs a free account.
- [zombifyai.com](https://zombifyai.com/) - About 20-second videos delivered by email within 12 hours, with no account needed.

## Tiptoeing in my jordans / Rumpelstiltskin dance

The trend: the "AI Rumpelstiltskin" clip set to "tiptoeing in my jordans", where a small figure shushes, grins and tiptoes across a barn.

- [ai-rumpelstiltskin.app](https://ai-rumpelstiltskin.app/) - 6- or 13-second Rumpelstiltskin dance videos; needs an account.
- [kavel.ai](https://www.kavel.ai/video/tip-toeing-in-my-jordans-ai) - A general AI video site with a 12-second 720p tip toeing in my jordans template.
- [rumpelmeme.com](https://rumpelmeme.com/) - 10-second 720p landscape AI videos, plus a free template editor that does not animate your face.
- [rumpelstiltskin-ai.app](https://rumpelstiltskin-ai.app/) - 10-second 720p videos by default, with 20 s, 30 s and 1080p options and a free 5-second preview for new accounts.
- [rumpelstiltskin-ai.com](https://www.rumpelstiltskin-ai.com/generator) - Several modes; the face-swap "take his place" mode makes 5-second videos, and the modes that keep the original dancer have free watermarked previews.
- [rumpelstiltskinai.app](https://rumpelstiltskinai.app/) - About 12-second landscape videos, with one free still portrait preview per Google account; usually 10 to 20 minutes per video.
- [rumpelstiltskinai.co](https://rumpelstiltskinai.co/) - 13-second 720p 16:9 videos; you give an email at checkout instead of creating an account.
- [rumpelstiltskinai.dance](https://rumpelstiltskinai.dance/) - 8-second 720p 16:9 videos, with the first 2 seconds free as a preview.
- [rumpelstiltskindance.org](https://rumpelstiltskindance.org/) - 10-second 1080p videos in 9:16 or 16:9, with sign-in or guest checkout.
- [rumpelstiltskinmeme.com](https://rumpelstiltskinmeme.com/) - Rumpelstiltskin meme videos in 6- or 13-second lengths; failed videos return their credits.
- [tiptoeing-in-my-jordans.com](https://tiptoeing-in-my-jordans.com/) - 10-second tiptoe dance video from one photo of a person or a pet, in 480p or 720p and 9:16, 16:9 or 1:1; you sign in when you pay. *(maintainer's site)*
- [tiptoeinginmyjordans.net](https://tiptoeinginmyjordans.net/) - 12-second videos up to 1080p, with no watermark.
- [tiptoeinginmyjordansai.com](https://tiptoeinginmyjordansai.com/) - 12-second silent 16:9 videos in 480p or 720p, with a free 2-second 480p preview for new accounts.

## Alors on danse duo dance

The trend: two people in a studio, one playing a keyboard while the other dances beside them.

- [alorsondansevideo.com](https://alorsondansevideo.com/) - 13-second 480p duo dance video from two portrait photos, in 16:9 or 9:16; no free option; independent site, not affiliated with Stromae or TikTok, and the original song is not included.

## Bad Times dance

The trend: a short dance clip of a person, a group or a pet, made from a single photo.

- [badtimesai.dev](https://badtimesai.dev/) - 14- to 15-second 480p vertical AI dance effect video from one portrait, group or pet photo, with five templates and no account needed; no free option; independent, unofficial site.

## I found you in the crowd

The trend: one person watches from a nightclub balcony, a spotlight finds the other in the crowd, and their eyes meet.

- [ifoundyouinthecrowdai.com](https://ifoundyouinthecrowdai.com/) - 10- to 15-second 480p vertical AI encounter effect video from two portrait photos, in three styles, with no account needed; no free option; independent, unofficial site.

## Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. Open an issue or a pull request with:

- the tool's URL and the trend it covers;
- one neutral sentence of facts that can be checked on the tool's own pages (length, resolution, free option, account);
- no prices, no superlatives, no affiliate or referral links.

Tools that are down, paused for a long time or no longer match the trend will be removed. If you run one of the listed tools and a fact is out of date, open an issue and it will be corrected.
